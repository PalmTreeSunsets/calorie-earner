Calorie Earner

Your calorie balance climbs in real time, all day and all night. Before you eat or drink anything, check the number and consume no more than it says. Every calorie gets earned before it gets spent.

How fast the balance climbs. Take what you'd normally eat to hold your weight and subtract the deficit you want — 500 a day below maintenance is the usual place to start.

You'll open with everything you'd have earned so far today already banked. This is applied on top of that — leave it at 0 for a clean start, or enter a negative number to cover what you've already eaten today.
