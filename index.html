Calorie Earner

Your calorie balance climbs in real time, all day and all night. Before you eat or drink anything, check the number and consume no more than it says. Every calorie gets earned before it gets spent.

How fast the balance climbs. You'll earn this many calories every 24 hours, one at a time, at a steady rate. Set it to the number of calories you want to consume each day. You can change this later.

The lowest allowed value is 1,200.

New entry

Enter the number of calories for what you consumed, then press Subtract. To put calories back instead, tap the − to switch it to +. To fix a mistake, remove the entry with × in the log.

Log

    The log keeps today and the two days before it. At midnight the oldest day drops off. A day dropping off never changes your balance. Removing an entry with × does change your balance, since that's a correction rather than time passing.

    Settings

    Changing this only changes the pace from this moment on. Calories you've already earned stay exactly where they are. The lowest allowed value is 1,200. Your starting calories per day was 2,000.

    Reset app

    Throws away your balance. Clears the log. Resets the app back to the startup screen. This can't be undone.
